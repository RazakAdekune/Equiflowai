EquiFlow is a structured investment platform that pools capital from verified investors and deploys it across a curated portfolio of high-yield assets. You deposit, we allocate — and your returns are tracked in real time through your investor dashboard.
Investment tiers start from $5,000 (Tier 1) and go up to $50,000 (Tier 4). Each tier comes with different return rates, priority access, and giveaway eligibility. All tiers qualify for the monthly Tesla draw.
Returns are calculated monthly and paid directly to your registered bank account or crypto wallet. You can also choose to reinvest your returns to compound your position. Payout schedules are visible on your dashboard at all times.
We operate under a strict risk architecture with layered portfolio diversification, stop-loss protocols, and capital reserve requirements. Investor funds are held in segregated accounts and never commingled with operational capital.
Every active investor with a confirmed deposit is automatically entered into our monthly Tesla draw — no tickets, no additional steps. The draw is held on the last day of each month and the winner is notified privately through the platform.
KYC verification is typically completed within 24–48 hours of submitting your documents. In most cases it's done within a few hours. You'll receive a confirmation email and platform notification as soon as your account is approved.
